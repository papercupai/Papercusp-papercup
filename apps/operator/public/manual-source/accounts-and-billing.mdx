Use this guide to confirm which account can access your workspace and who
pays its costs. You should finish able to identify the intended organization,
check workspace access, and distinguish a pending payment from a confirmed
subscription before paying again.

You can read this manual without signing in. Acting on a hosted workspace
requires the correct Papercusp account and organization access. An external
integration connection and a paid subscription are separate from sign-in.

Before starting checkout, identify the organization you
intend to pay for and check whether your role can manage its billing.

## Create an account once

Choose **Create account**, verify the identity offered by the sign-up flow,
review the terms and privacy notice, and return to the portal.
For an existing identity, choose **Sign in** using the same provider.
If account creation says the identity is already registered, repeated
sign-ups will not repair access.

Use the account and organization that own the intended workspace. Being
signed in does not grant access to every project or another organization's
machines.

Open **Portal account** using the account icon in the navigation rail. Check
the displayed identity and **Status**. The **Accounts sidebar** manages the
selected workspace's model accounts; it does not identify your Papercusp
sign-in or grant organization membership.

If the portal does not show an organization name or selector, ask the intended
organization's administrator to confirm your membership and which workspace
you should be able to open. If that workspace is missing, confirm your sign-in
identity and ask the administrator to check your access. Signing up again
does not grant missing workspace permission.

## Connect integrations separately

Gmail, Calendar and other integrations request their own authorization.
They do not create a Papercusp owner session, and disconnecting one should
not be confused with signing out of Papercusp.

Review provider permissions before connecting. Never include verification
links, session cookies, passwords or tokens in a support report.
See [Plugins](/docs/plugins) and [Remote access](/docs/remote-access).

## Use the billing page shown for your account

If your installation offers hosted subscriptions, open **Portal account**
and choose **Billing** while signed in. Read the current plans, status and
restrictions. Use the plan and price displayed there; this guide does not
promise a fixed price, allowance or provider quota. Local installations may
show a different, read-only billing state.

There are three separate cost categories: your Papercusp subscription, model
usage billed to the applicable model account, and cloud resources billed under
the selected hosting arrangement. Read the current account and provider terms
for the amounts and payer. Stopping agents or closing the browser does not
cancel a subscription or remove cloud resources. See
[Cloud workspaces](/docs/cloud-workspaces#stop-delete-and-recover-deliberately) before
stopping or deleting a host.

Choose the displayed subscription action to open checkout.
After returning, wait for the portal's subscription confirmation.
The checkout return page alone is not proof that payment and entitlements
have been confirmed.

If **Manage billing** is available, it opens the supported billing-management
flow. Review its effective dates and cancellation consequences before making
changes. Billing controls can depend on your role in the organization.

## Payment pending or an unexpected restriction

Read the status message rather than starting duplicate subscriptions.
Record the time and the status message shown in Billing. If confirmation or
access remains wrong, use Support.

A subscription does not prove that cloud provisioning succeeded, and an
active plan does not imply unlimited resources. Check the workspace's status
and the limits actually shown by your installation.
See [Cloud workspaces](/docs/cloud-workspaces) and
[Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).

## Expected result

You have checked the displayed sign-in identity and confirmed your organization
membership and intended workspace through the portal or its administrator.
Verify workspace access separately, and check for a confirmed subscription
status if you purchased one. If the status remains pending, record the time
and displayed status and follow the recovery above before retrying payment.

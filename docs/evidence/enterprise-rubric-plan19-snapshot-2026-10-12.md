## Background

The owner asked what enterprise customers will want that Papercusp does not yet have, then requested: "Write this into a plan and then show me a detailed summary."

Source assessment: [Enterprise features Papercusp should prioritize](/reports/rpt_11e890f4-7249-43a2-9679-fb3584596f02), report rpt_11e890f4-7249-43a2-9679-fb3584596f02, research WI-10007819. Drafting task: WI-10007824.

The assessment identifies eight capability areas: directory/group administration, organization-wide agent policy, comprehensive data governance, compliance/audit export, department budgets and ROI, supported enterprise connectors, approved departmental workflows, and managed deployment/support. Procurement assurance is an associated operational program.

Existing foundations include WorkOS authentication and lifecycle revocation; local memberships and grants; capability/autonomy/sandbox controls; organization documents and source ACLs; disclosure labels; chat retention/legal holds; budget enforcement and billing; plans/recipes/templates; desktop distribution; backups; and AWS/GCP customer-cloud hosting. Presence in source is distinct from supported deployment availability. Product priorities remain hypotheses until validated with buyers.

Related work to reuse rather than reopen: enterprise-data-sources-2026-10-01 and hosted-byoc-confidentiality-correction-2026-10-02. Both were read as status shipped on 2026-10-09. Their implementations and acceptance evidence must be checked by the capability census; their shipped status does not alone prove every target installation supports the same property. This roadmap adds organization administration, integrated enterprise packaging, business acceptance, and operational commitments across multiple subsystems, rather than duplicating either narrower plan.

## Outcome and scope

Build an enterprise offering that lets IT administer identity and policy, security control data and audit actions, finance bound and allocate spending, and departments run approved workflows with accepted results.

The recommended first release combines directory/group administration, central policy, security/audit export, minimum data-governance controls, enforceable budgets, basic recovery/support readiness, and one buyer-selected connector/workflow. Deeper compliance, more connectors, full e-discovery, customer-managed keys, and broad managed rollout are later packages unless the selected customer makes them a prerequisite.

The roadmap is organized by dependency and acceptance gate, not a promised calendar. Duration, staffing, commercial commitments, model/account choices, and execution route remain open.

## Product shaping

Audience: enterprise IT/security administrators, finance owners and department reviewers who need to govern useful agent workflows across teams. The proposed first segment is developer-tools or infrastructure support/engineering; this remains a hypothesis until attributable buyer evidence is captured in P-002.

Problem: existing identity, local grants, policy, data-source ACLs, disclosure, audit, budgets, plans/recipes and hosting are foundations, but source presence does not establish a complete supported enterprise administration and workflow product. Enterprise qualification must join these controls into one selected deployment and business journey, with explicit costs, recovery and accountable owners.

Outcome: a bounded enterprise profile in which administrators manage directory/groups and inherited policy; employees run one permitted departmental workflow; reviewers accept or reject its result; security can follow allowed and denied actions; finance bounds and reconciles cost; operators install, recover and roll back the exact supported artifact. The core and advanced packages follow D-003 and the existing item graph.

Non-goals: duplicate shipped data-source/confidentiality foundations; silently qualify every IdP, connector, cloud, region or MDM combination; infer customer acceptance, legal compliance, certification, support commitments or shipment from source/demo evidence; contact or recruit prospects without the corresponding authorized activity. Advanced controls required by a buyer become explicit prerequisites, not implicit exclusions.

Scenarios: (1) An allowed employee prepares a cited support-to-engineering handoff for a distinct department reviewer, under approved sources/providers and a finite budget. (2) A removed group member or deactivated user loses access across the enumerated transports within the selected bound; permitted users still work. (3) Lower-scope overrides, expired grants/exceptions, self-approval, restricted-source disclosure and exhausted/unbounded cost admission fail closed and leave auditable evidence. (4) An administrator restores or rolls back the candidate profile while preserving selected identity, policy, authorization and data obligations.

Facts versus choices: P-001 supplies the source/reuse census; P-002's discovery packet and the P-003 pilot-contract preparation draft are hypotheses and proposed contracts. Real buyer/workflow evidence, selected scope/thresholds, independent acceptance-rubric review, partner trials, qualified external inputs and final artifact verification remain missing gates. Complete preparation is in docs/evidence/enterprise-pilot-contract-draft-2026-10-11.md and verified owner report rpt_37cd3abd-3f72-4c2d-b8ca-8840f7a9d790. No requirement, item dependency, BAR or completion state is waived by this shaping section.

## Design

### Reuse and integration

Extend the WorkOS/provider-neutral identity seam and local grant authority for directory administration. Add approved group mappings and administrative UX to the existing identity/authorization path.

Expose organization policy through existing capability, autonomy, plugin-grant, sandbox, and data-disclosure machinery. The capability census must identify actual enforcement points in every selected execution backend and transport before policy inheritance can be claimed.

Extend the organization corpus/source ACLs, disclosure ledger, connector/source records, retention/hold mechanisms, billing budget reservations, audit/decision ledgers, plans/recipes/templates, cloud hosting, distribution, and backup surfaces. Do not introduce a parallel enterprise auth service, data corpus, policy engine, agent loop, billing ledger, or workflow runner by default.

Design new admin/workflow screens through design-phase.search_registry, specification, and design-phase.validate_spec/lint_spec before UI implementation. A new standalone app, if later justified, follows the maintained Papercusp app-template flow.

### Proposed release packages

Enterprise core: identity/groups, inherited policy, sensitive-action approval, pilot data contract, action evidence/SIEM export, finite budgets, basic operational recovery, one supported connector/workflow, and reviewable procurement/support terms.

Expansion: a governed workflow catalog, additional demand-backed connectors, delegated finance, cost allocation, outcome dashboards, and repeated customer acceptance.

Regulated and managed estate: region/provider restrictions across data paths, customer-controlled key management, complete retention/e-discovery scope, MDM and update rings, private-network deployment validation, independent security assurance, recovery objectives, and support/SLA obligations.

Optional advanced capabilities become pilot blockers only when a customer prerequisite is explicitly recorded. Core confidentiality, authorization, bounded spending, and necessary recovery are never postponed as "later packaging."

### Acceptance examples and measurements

Identity: a user in an approved Finance group receives only the approved grants; removing the group closes access within the documented bound; an unapproved upstream role cannot elevate access.

Policy: a workspace-local setting cannot bypass an organization restriction; an expired exception stops permitting the action; an approver cannot satisfy both sides of a required separation-of-duties check.

Data: revoking a source permission blocks later retrieval and applicable disclosure; canary restricted content cannot exit through the selected model, tool, connector, log, export, or support path; restore preserves intended restrictions.

Audit: an action and its approval/policy references can be exported to the chosen SIEM without secrets; duplicate export/replay does not duplicate events; evidence tampering is detected by the selected mechanism.

Finance: concurrent department requests cannot overspend the parent allocation; cancellation and missing provider bills cannot imply zero cost; an accepted workflow result includes costs, missing-measurement labels, review effort, and rework.

Workflow: an employee can complete the selected journey using an approved version, and a department owner can accept/reject the result using the stated business criterion.

Deployment: the supported installation can upgrade and restore using documented controls; negative authorization/tenant-isolation cases fail; the corresponding legitimate journeys succeed.

Evidence receipts must pin code/artifact, policy/schema version, test/runtime profile, procedure, outcome, and limitations. Numeric pilot success, cost, review-effort, support-load, revocation, recovery, and stop thresholds are proposed in P-003 and agreed before trials; no invented performance or adoption numbers are treated as measured.

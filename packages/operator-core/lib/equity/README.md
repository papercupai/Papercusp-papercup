# OpenZeppelin equity software

This extends the existing pot tool, wallet-binding, reconciliation RPC and authentication surfaces. `pot:equity` is a focused ABI adapter because no existing tool prepares ownership contracts. It creates no custody service, payment rail, database, app scaffold or replacement for P2P plan admission.

The software implements issuer-attested actual ownership. A formed issuer, binding equity terms, lawful offering/transfer rules and a legally authoritative register must be established externally. The immutable `issuerCommitment` must hash the exact signed issuer documents, including token/register recognition, rights, recovery and rounding rules. The issuer wallet explicitly activates genesis and each registered holder accepts that same commitment. The contract records attestation; it cannot establish legal effectiveness, regulatory eligibility, market liquidity or enterprise value. The example test issuer and URL are fixtures only.

Deploy the pinned Cancun artifacts with `pot:equity prepare`: (1) PapercuspEquity(commitment, registerURI, supplyCap, genesisShares); (2) EquityTimelock(delaySeconds >= 86400); (3) EquityGovernor(equity, timelock). The issuer wallet calls activateGenesis(identityCommitment, commitment), initializeGovernor(governor) on the timelock, then bindGovernance(governor, timelock) on equity. Verify addresses and roles before signing. A bootstrap has a fixed genesis allotment and one-time wiring power, never an additional mint or role-change path after binding. Non-upgradeable contracts deliberately support bounded policy amendment rather than arbitrary code replacement.

Use `cupboard:bind-wallet` for the principal-to-wallet association. `pot:equity` requires an authenticated principal and prepares unsigned transactions only; the actual wallet signs and the contracts enforce authority. It accepts no private keys and never broadcasts. Large constructors/ABIs are paged before result truncation: concatenate chunks in nextOffset order, verify totalChars and SHA-256 over the exact concatenated UTF-8 hex text, then submit the assembled transaction to the wallet. A partial page is not a transaction. `inspect` uses the existing PAPERCUSP_RECONCILIATION_CHAIN_RPC_URL injected outside the repository, verifies chain id, and pins code and state reads to one block. Code presence and ABI-readable state alone do not prove source authenticity; inspect labels that uncertainty explicitly. Verify deployment source/bytecode independently.

Governance approves registration/eligibility, bounded grants, grant termination and share returns. New holders must accept terms personally. Whole shares use zero decimals. Identity commitments must uniquely represent real eligible people; issuance administrators must verify them, because a hash does not prove unique human membership. There are at most 256 registered identities, bounding return enumeration/gas. This implementation is appropriate to a bounded company membership, not an unlimited public share register.

Both economic voting power and individual registered-holder approval are required. A transfer/issuance eligibility freeze preserves existing accepted positive-balance owners' ballots, including when every holder is frozen; governance can therefore restore eligibility through the same dual approval and timelock. Issuer terms must recognize that distinction: this contract freezes transfers and new-share claims rather than suspending ownership votes. Token votes self-delegate on receipt; delegation can change economic voting power but never creates additional individual member ballots. Each proposal freezes the prior approval threshold. Defaults are 60% for ordinary actions, 75% for amendments, 60 seconds before the voting snapshot, one day voting, and at least one day queued delay. Policy changes, grant-limit changes and timelock-delay changes use the protected threshold even in mixed batches. Immutable floors are >50% ordinary, 66.67% protected, 60 seconds voting delay, one hour voting, one day execution delay. No arbitrary calls/payments, role grants, timelock replacement, relay or proxy upgrade are admitted. A recovery preserves member identity so one proposal cannot receive two member ballots. Wallet recovery can move current economic delegation; pending economic votes remain tied to the original address snapshot and are not recreated on the new wallet.

Grants reserve the full committed issuance against the immutable cap, are linear over explicit timestamps, and cannot reuse a commitment id. Maximum per-grant allotment starts at 10% of the cap; protected governance may reduce/restore that prospective limit within the same bound. Termination releases only unearned future shares; earned and already issued ownership survives. Claiming requires current eligibility, so a legal freeze suspends claims without deleting earned rights. Governance must re-enable eligibility to resume them.

Returns are additional equity, never cash dividends. Governance declares a fixed prior-second record date. Per-identity entitlements equal floor(record-date shares × bps / 10000); exact entitlements are reserved without unclaimable aggregate rounding dust. Rights survive sales and consented recovery; new shares cannot capture old returns and each identity claims once. Defaults bound issuance to 10% across a 365-day window with at least 30 days between declarations. These are issuance limits, not a guarantee or automation of profits. A proportionate share bonus preserves percentages when all eligible holders receive and claim it; issuance does not by itself increase company value. Changing an eligibility population can change those percentages. The cap covers issued shares plus every outstanding grant and return commitment.

Recovery requires old-wallet or previously designated recovery-agent authorization and acceptance by the new wallet of the issuer terms. It moves balances and keeps stable grant/return identities; the old wallet cannot receive new shares or reuse that identity. The issuer/governor has no unilateral seizure/recovery power. If no consent or agent was recorded, this contract has no lost-key override.

## Version 2: HR grants with a bounded admin (dao-hr-people-2026-10-11 D-004, D-008, D-013)

`PapercuspEquityV2` and `EquityGovernorV2` are a second contract pair for DAO HR. The V1 contracts stay as they are. In V2 the members vote only on hiring and firing; routine operations go to a bounded admin role with no vote.

- **Vote required** (only the timelock, after a passed member proposal): `registerHolder` + `commitGrant` (hire), `stopGrant` (fire; earned equity stays), `setEligibility` (legal freeze), `replaceAdmin` (lost or compromised admin). `EquityGovernorV2` refuses a proposal for anything else.
- **Admin, no vote, bounded**: `transferAdmin`; `setGrantLimit` (at most the immutable `maxGrantLimit`); `setReturnPolicy` (rate at most `ANNUAL_RETURN_CAP_BPS` = 10% per 365 days, interval at least `MIN_RETURN_INTERVAL` = 30 days); `raiseGrant` (an unvested grant, within the limit); `triggerPayout` (only when due and within the ceiling).
- **The caller's own position only**: `claimGrant`, `resignGrant` (resignation: the holder stops their own vesting and keeps what they earned; no vote), `claimReturn`, terms, recovery, transfers and delegation.

Grants have a cliff: nothing is earned before it. The return ceiling and minimum interval are compile-time constants with no setter. `v2-function-classes.ts` is the caller-class table; `equity-v2.test.ts` checks it against the compiled ABI, makes hostile calls into every governed function, and fuzzes call sequences to show that none of them lowers earned equity. `inspectEquity` reads the deployed token's ERC-5267 domain version and selects the V1 or V2 equity/governor ABI for its state reads.

To show a mutated `.sol` to the EVM tests (for example with `scripts/mutation-probe.sh`), set `PAPERCUSP_EQUITY_COMPILE_FROM_SOURCE=1`. The helpers then compile the sources in memory instead of reading `artifacts.json`, which takes about 2 minutes per run.

Verification lives in compile.test.ts, equity.test.ts, equity-v2.test.ts, governor.test.ts and runtime.test.ts. It compiles pinned OpenZeppelin 5.6.1 with solc 0.8.37 and executes signed transactions on EthereumJS 10.1.3 in Cancun mode, including hostile calls. Run the registered suites with the root `npm run test:file -- packages/operator-core/lib/equity/compile.test.ts packages/operator-core/lib/equity/equity.test.ts packages/operator-core/lib/equity/governor.test.ts packages/operator-core/lib/equity/runtime.test.ts`. Software shipment is distinct from issuer activation or public-chain deployment.

## Work-item review is separate from plan approval

The maintained policy chooses **reproduction** for bugs and **proposal review** for changes/features/tasks. Pending review on a bug is a validation/intake state, not a demand that Avi approve the plan again. The 748 review-floor rows split into 595 pending, 152 revision-requested and 1 retired readiness-only reason. None has a stored filing or intake reproduction receipt in these snapshots. Blocking implementation of an unvalidated report makes sense; blocking the work that validates it does not.

All 797 pending/revision records across the cohort are well formed and ledger-linked. Every one of the 152 revision rows in the other bucket has feedback. The maintained resubmission routes cover 96 provenance-recoverable sweep submissions, 39 orphan-adoption cases and 2 original-submitter cases; 15 have unresolved submitter authority. Existing source supports reviewer claims and evidence-driven orphan adoption. Age and a grade alone do not establish bug acceptance. Of 605 pending records across all categories, 81 are older than 24 hours, 52 are younger, and 472 lack usable current-round timing.

## Assigned and claim-held do not mean working

**496 + 279 is not 775 actively worked bugs.** Item-specific tool/progress reads and owner-bound native transcript reads give this complete partition of the 496 assigned rows:

- 4 have an aborted native turn with the reservation retained.
- 2 have a disappeared holder process.
- 9 changed assignment or became terminal during the later read.
- 61 lack usable owner-bound native attestation, 49 have a bounded native tail without affirmative current item-work evidence, and 1 has an open turn without demonstrated item progress.

The former 314 suspect rows were drilled down: 246 completed turns, 4 aborted turns, 2 disappeared processes, 1 changed row, 1 with recent item work, 57 without usable native attestation, 2 without affirmative item work in the native tail, and 1 open turn without demonstrated item progress. The raw suspect label is not the final finding. Missing evidence is explicitly not evidence of inactivity. Instrument gaps and reservation settlement are owned under WI-10008019.

Of the 279 claim-held rows, 276 are unassigned and the 3 assigned rows have no affirmative recent item-work proof in the scoped read. This category primarily describes paused work.

## Holds that need correction

Across the overlapping 385 durable parks, 149 lack a trigger. Of the 52 saved satisfied flags, 47 relied on generic wakes; the current specific-condition resolver does not accept them as proof. Specific event latches can also be stale or contradict a pause/resume condition. For the 278 parks in the primary claim-held bucket: 92 have no trigger, 79 have a registered condition, 58 have an unverified specific condition, 46 have a specific fired latch and 3 are unreachable. These are audit findings, not authorization to clear those holds.

Across the 440 blocked rows, the event/contract assessment found 10 registered pending events, 2 fired events needing condition recheck, 5 expired/unreachable events, 8 missing clearer actions and 6 missing evidence records; the remaining external records require actual runtime/prose-condition verification. These axes overlap items. The 41 dependency edges over 36 rows have 28 satisfied and 13 unsatisfied edges; satisfied edges do not remove other holds.

All 6 recovery-window rows had their detector keys absent from six complete ran ticks. They need the maintained auto-close/recovery settlement, not speculative implementation. The admission promoter was not stalled at the read; admission exclusions must still respect the maintained age/fail-open bound.
